# Judicial Asset Seizure and Judgment Execution

This skill provides an operational framework for legal judgment execution, enabling understanding of asset discovery, legal instruments, and liquidation processes.

**NOTE:** This skill is for operational awareness and framework documentation of established legal processes. It does not constitute legal advice or authorization to execute extra-legal actions. All actions must comply with jurisdictional law and court orders.

## Asset Discovery (Phase 2)

Locating the debtor's attachable assets (bank accounts, real estate, vehicles, or business inventory). Courts can issue:

- **Interrogatories/Depositions**: Forcing the debtor to answer questions regarding their finances under oath.
- **Subpoenas**: Demanding financial records from third parties (e.g., banks, employers, or business partners).

## Execution Instruments (Phase 3)

Mechanisms filed with the court to initiate the seizure:

- **Writ of Execution**: A court order directing local law enforcement to physically seize the debtor's property.
- **Bank Garnishment (Non-Wage Garnishment)**: An order served directly to the debtor's bank, compelling the institution to freeze the account and surrender funds.
- **Wage Garnishment**: A directive requiring the employer to withhold a percentage of earnings.
- **Real Estate Liens**: A legal claim attached to the debtor's property title.
- **Till Tap / Keeper Levy**: Law enforcement is dispatched to physically empty a business's cash register or collect daily revenue.

## Asset Liquidation (Phase 4)

If physical property is seized (Writ of Execution), law enforcement holds a public auction (Sheriff's Sale). Proceeds, minus fees, are applied to the judgment balance.
